How it works

You tap who's here. The app finds ways to split exactly those people into tables, so that as many people as possible play something they want to play. Then it shows you the best options, best first.

⭐ Where the data comes from

  • Your ratings come from our Google Sheet: 2 = want to play, 1 = would play, 0 = rather not, empty = didn't say. The app reloads the sheet every time you open it.
  • Who can explain a game (📖) and who brought it are also from the sheet.
  • Player counts, play time and how heavy a game is come from BoardGameGeek. If the sheet has its own player counts for a game, the sheet wins.

📱 The main screen

  • Who's playing? Tap names on or off. Everyone who's on gets exactly one seat. Nobody is left over.
  • Up to N tables. The most tables you have room for. Fewer is fine if that works better.
  • ⏱ any length. Only show tables that take at least or at most a certain time, e.g. "at most 1½ h" before dinner.
  • Games. The number on a game is how many people here want it (rated 2). Tap once: 📌 must be played. Tap again: ⊘ leave it out. Tap again: normal. Hold a game to choose who plays it.
  • 📊 opens the stats page, the settings and the full game list.

If BGG has no player-count votes for a game, the app falls back to the next wider range (best → recommended → what the box says). If there's nothing at all, it assumes 2–4.

✋ Overruling the rules

  • 📌 Must be played: the game is in every result. It ignores "owner is here" and the time slot. If you pin more games than you have tables, extra tables are added.
  • ✓ This person plays it (hold the game, or tap its name in a result): they sit there even if they rated it 0. If the normal rules leave no way to play it with them, player count and explainer rules are dropped for that game.
  • ✕ Never this person: they're never seated at that game.
  • 🔒 Lock a table (lock button in a result): that exact table, same game, same people, stays put while you change other things.

Your own choices always beat the automatic rules. If they contradict each other (say you pinned a game nobody here may play), the app tells you and offers a fix.

🏆 How results are ordered

  1. Most happiness: every seat earns points: 2 for a game you want, 1 for "would play". The percentage is how close a result gets to "everyone plays a 2".
  2. Fewer guesses: on a tie, results that rely less on empty ratings come first.
  3. Tables ending together: on a tie after that, results whose tables finish closer together come first.

You see the best 30 different game combinations. Each game combination appears once, with its best seating.

Cater to picky players (settings, off by default): a "2" from someone who rarely gives 2s counts a bit more when ordering. The percentages shown stay plain ratings.

🔍 Reading a result

  • #1 87 %: rank and score.
  • Clock: green = tables end at about the same time, amber = one runs clearly longer, red = one runs much longer.
  • ~2 h next to a game: the estimated time for that table. Bold is the longest table, amber or red means much longer than the shortest.
  • 📖 next to a name: can explain it. 🎓 next to the time: everyone at the table has played it before, so no rules explanation.
  • Seatings: same games, other ways to split the people. Tap "Use" to take one.
  • Share: sends the result to the group chat. It asks first whether it's really decided.

⏳ Play times

  • Start from BGG's box time, adjusted for how many sit at the table.
  • If anyone at the table is new to the game, add time to explain it (more for heavier games) and a little extra because newcomers play slower.
  • If everyone has played it (🎓), no explaining and only a small buffer.
  • "Played it before" comes from the explainers, the sheet's Kennen column, and what you tick under Already played it in a game.
  • Times are rounded to ¾ h, 1 h, 1½ h, 2 h, 3 h, 4 h, 5 h+. They're estimates, not promises. Exact minutes: settings → "Show minutes".

🔒 Your phone only

  • Everything you tap (who's here, pins, picks, settings) is saved only on your phone. Nobody else sees it, and your choices don't change anyone else's app.
  • To show the group, use the share button.
  • Ratings only change in the Google Sheet.
  • Settings → Reset everything starts fresh.

📄 Using your own sheet

Any Google Sheet tab with the same layout works. Settings → Data → Use another sheet, then paste the link to the tab. Only your phone switches. "Back to the retreat sheet" switches back.

  • Sharing: the sheet must be shared as "Anyone with the link: Viewer".
  • Which tab: the one in your link (the #gid=… part). Cell A1 is used as its name.
  • Header row: anywhere in the first 8 rows. Rows above it are free for notes.
  • One column per person: the header is the name, the cells are 0, 1, 2 or empty. People who haven't rated yet still count.
  • Columns starting with * are ignored, apart from the ones below.

🤷 No result?

Some groups just don't split under the current rules, e.g. 7 people with only 4-player games. The app then suggests what to loosen: one more table, "would play" games too, any player count, and so on. Tap one to try it.
